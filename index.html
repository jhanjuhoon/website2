The ritual

Digital is useful. Film is the part I look forward to. Loading a canister in the shade, winding until the leader catches, then spending an afternoon on one street instead of a thousand thumbnails.

  • Load

    A fresh roll, a light-tight back, and the tiny prayer that the sprockets actually caught.

  • Walk

    Corners, bus stops, late sun on brick. I wait for a frame instead of spraying and hoping.

  • Wait

    The scan email days later is still the best notification I get all week.

What I carry

Canon AE-1

A 1970s SLR with a shutter that still sounds expensive. Manual enough to teach me, automatic enough to keep up.

50mm f/1.8

One lens, most days. It forces me closer and keeps the bag light enough to forget I am “out shooting.”

A notebook

Light, place, what I thought I saw. When the scans come back, the notes are half the fun.
